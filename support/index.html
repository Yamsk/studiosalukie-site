Need help with Emerji? We're a small studio and we read every message.

Contact us

Email us and we'll get back to you as soon as we can:

support@studiosalukie.com

To help us sort it out faster, tell us your device (e.g. iPhone 14, Pixel 7), your OS version, and a short description of the problem.

Common questions

  • Lost progress or restoring purchases. Your progress is saved on your device. If you reinstalled or switched devices, open the in-game Store and tap Restore Purchases to recover anything you bought.
  • Ads & privacy choices. You can review or change your ad-consent and privacy options any time from Settings → Privacy Options inside the game.
  • Delete my data. See the data deletion request section of our Privacy Policy.
  • Something's broken. Email us with the details above and we'll look into it.
